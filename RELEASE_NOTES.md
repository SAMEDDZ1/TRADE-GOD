# TRADE GOD v0.1.0

The first TRADE GOD build: a multi-agent trading company on your desktop, on a paper account.

## What is in it

- **The trading desk.** Chart, watchlist, order ticket, positions, approvals, crew scorecards, backtest lab, journal and
  setup, in one window that opens when TRADE GOD starts. Its first tab is the pixel-art TRADING FLOOR.
- **A 36-role crew** in seven departments — from the Head Coach and the Agent Coach to the Metals Specialist, the Bull
  and Bear advocates and the Risk Manager. Every crew member can read the market and propose trades; none can place
  an order.
- **A risk engine in plain code.** A stop-loss on every trade, 1% risk per trade, 3% daily loss, 5 positions, 5% total
  risk, 10:1 leverage and Canada's leverage caps — all by default. Raising a limit needs you to type a confirmation.
- **The Trading Council.** A Bull and a Bear argue every crew idea before it reaches you.
- **The Agent Coach.** Bots that trade badly go to review, retraining, probation (half size), practice (paper only)
  or the bench — by rules, not moods. Promotions are yours to accept.
- **A paper account** with real spreads, slippage on gapped stops, and a one-click kill switch.
- **A backtester** with costs on, next-bar fills, walk-forward overfitting checks and Monte Carlo drawdowns.
- **TradingView alerts** turn into proposals (secret-protected, stop required). TradingView CSV exports feed the lab.
- **OANDA practice** for real forex, gold, silver and index prices (connector untested against the live service).
- **A tamper-evident journal** of every proposal, veto, approval, fill and close.

## Not in this release

- Live trading with a real broker. Every order goes to the paper account.
- Connectors for Interactive Brokers, Alpaca, tastytrade, Coinbase and Kraken (planned). Pepperstone, IC Markets,
  Binance and Bybit are locked for Canadian residents.
- Trade approvals from your phone (planned).
